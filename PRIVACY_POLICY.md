# Privacy Policy — Pencraft

**Last updated:** 2026-11-01  
**App:** Pencraft — Invoice & Estimate Maker  
**Developer:** Isaac (`com.isaac.pencraft`)

---

## 1. What data we collect

Pencraft stores all your data **locally on your device only**.

| Data | Where stored | Purpose |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Business profile (name, address, logo) | Device only | Printed on your invoices |
| Client names, emails, phone numbers | Device only | Address invoices to clients |
| Invoice & estimate content | Device only | Your business records |
| Payment records | Device only | Track what you've been paid |
| App preferences (theme, currency) | Device only | Personalise your experience |

## 2. What we do NOT collect

- We do **not** collect any analytics or usage data.
- We do **not** upload your invoices, client data, or business information to any server.
- We do **not** share any of your data with third parties.
- We do **not** use your data for advertising.

## 3. Advertising (Free tier)

The free tier displays ads powered by **Google AdMob**. Google may collect device identifiers and approximate location to serve contextually relevant ads. You can review Google's privacy policy at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Upgrading to **Pencraft Pro** removes all ads.

## 4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed through **Google Play Billing**. Pencraft does not store your payment card details. Purchase history is managed by Google Play.

## 5. Backup files

When you use the Backup feature, a `.json` file is saved to a location you choose on your device. This file contains all your Pencraft data. Pencraft has no access to this file once you save it. Sharing this file with others is at your discretion.

## 6. Permissions

| Permission | Why we need it |
|-----------|---------------|
| `INTERNET` | AdMob ads (free tier) and in-app purchase validation |
| `READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Save backup files and PDF exports (Android < 10) |

## 7. Children

Pencraft is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be noted in the app update release notes.
